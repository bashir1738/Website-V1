BLOCKFUSE LABS — PARTNER LOGO PACK
Collected 27 September 2026

25 organisation/ecosystem identities; 30 source files including variants.

CONTENTS
- partners/: source logos for documented partners/collaborators.
- prodfest-supporters/: BGA and Plateau State Government artwork.
- hosted-ecosystems/: Celo, Sui, and Scroll. An event at Blockfuse does not establish an ongoing institutional partnership.
- event-collaborators/: general Avalanche logo. This is not a Team1 Nigeria-specific logo.
- programme-context/: Solana Foundation logo; credit the Solana Foundation Nigeria Grants Programme for the documented award.
- png/: PNG renderings of every source file, retaining original artwork and colour. Vector sources are rendered at up to 1200 pixels wide; raster sources are not enlarged. Transparency is preserved when present in the source.
- SOURCES.csv: exact file provenance, dimensions, and quality notes.
- STILL_NEEDED.txt: remaining logos that could not be reliably downloaded or identified.

USE
Prefer original SVG files on your website. White variants need a dark background. Retain aspect ratios and clear space. Some originals have opaque backgrounds; they have not been removed or reconstructed.
Original logos were downloaded or their vector paths extracted from organisation websites. Uniswap Foundation's CSS colour was resolved to the exact site value #05041f. Base and The Graph files come from their official brand kits. Starknet Africa and Superteam Nigeria use the actual artwork from their Luma profiles. Sunnah Tech Lab comes from NNIA's member directory; NACOS UniJos comes from a GDG on Campus UniJos event partner listing. See SOURCES.csv for each case.

nHub's available website PNG is small: request a higher-resolution original for large display. Profile art is not a substitute for a full brand kit. The pack is not evidence that every organisation is a current funder or that logo-use permission has been granted. Keep the relationship categories established in the research, and follow the respective brand guidelines.

No logo has been generated, traced, or redrawn. A preview background is used only in the contact sheet, not baked into the individual PNG exports.
